# FoldGPT public media

- The application icon is a direct vector transcription of FoldGPT Android's foldgpt_icon.xml: same three paths and colors, no substitute logo.
- foldgpt-social.png is a faithful 1280 × 480 raster export of the public repository's docs/assets/foldgpt-banner.svg. It uses the julienpiron.fr brand and the exact application icon. It is vector artwork rendered with CairoSVG, not AI-generated imagery, a photograph, or a device demonstration.
- codex-desktop-anonymized.png is a genuine development-device capture with two limited, direct pixel edits authorized by the owner: the account name is replaced with the illustrative placeholder `you@gmail.com`, and the personal avatar with a neutral user pictogram. The placeholder is not the actual account identity. Every pixel outside those two small account regions matches the original capture, including the complete composer, model selector and access controls. No generative image model was used. It replaces the earlier version with oversized black masks.
- workspace-runtime.png is an unmodified development-device capture of workspace settings.
- android-keyboard.png is an unmodified development-device capture of a Samsung split-keyboard input test.
- The gallery images are historical screenshots, not a connected browser session or a video demonstration. The interactive handset is a WebGL visualization, with the account-anonymized Fold capture on the inner display and the separately credited Remote preview on the cover display. It is not a photographic device recording.
- `chatgpt-remote-official.png` is a pixel-preserving crop of [OpenAI's official Remote documentation preview](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/remote), captured on 11 September 2026. It shows the task-list scenario; the iPhone frame and system status area are excluded. This is OpenAI's illustration of the mobile Remote interface, not a screenshot captured from Android, the development Fold or a live connection. Computer names and task examples are OpenAI's original demonstration content. No interface, text or status was invented or changed for FoldGPT; no generative image editing was used. The source browser screenshot is 1280 × 720; the unchanged crop is `[574, 138, 926, 710]` (352 × 572). Image SHA-256: `7c610c24b4c4bc868389b27465375e2bcdde10aae135e5126cf08a10455eb50f`.
- ChatGPT/Codex interface and Samsung keyboard marks belong to their respective owners; shown for interoperability documentation. FoldGPT is independent.
- Handset geometry is a code-rendered illustration of the standard Galaxy Z Fold8 (SM-F971B). Camera count and placement were checked against [Samsung's product page](https://www.samsung.com/fr/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/) and its [rear-camera reference image](https://images.samsung.com/fr/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/images/galaxy-z-fold8-features-summary-camera.jpg) on 11 September 2026. The two cameras belong to the half behind the right side of the inner display; viewed from the rear, that half appears on the left and the cover display on the right. No Samsung product photograph is embedded in the handset.
